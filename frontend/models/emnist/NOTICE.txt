EMNIST-byclass TensorFlow.js layers model (62 classes: 0-9, A-Z, a-z).

Converted from the MIT-licensed training script in
https://github.com/4ltrem/emnist-web-app (model.json + group1-shard1of1.bin).

Dataset: EMNIST ByClass (NIST Special Database 19 / Cohen et al.).
Input: 28x28 grayscale, ink=1 on black=0, matching extract_training_samples('byclass')
which stores characters transposed relative to a normal screen glyph — sofianotes
transposes the raster before inference.
